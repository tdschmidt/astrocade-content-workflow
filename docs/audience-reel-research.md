## What changes

The previous default already contained several of those examples. Replacing examples alone will not fix an ordinary sorting/cleaning selection followed by a literal description. Earlier clips such as an SUV reaching “enlightenment” or an Iron Golem getting paid can be grounded and readable without matching this audience. They are technical playback/edit evidence, not proof that the content direction succeeded.

Selection now prefers a recognizable character or cultural collision **with several actual playable parts**: for example, choosing a form, using its ability, encountering a new obstacle and showing the outcome. Three similar hits split into separate shots are not three meaningful parts. A famous title pasted onto a shallow reskin is also insufficient. Shortlist goals identify provisional parts to inspect; actual play must establish them before editing.

The default requests a reel of3–6 meaningful shots within15seconds, preserving useful action and consequence rather than only one tiny event. It may contain several stages of one loop; it does not require unrelated mechanics. Saved briefs without the new optional `editingStyle` retain episode behavior. Fresh culture-focused briefs use `reel`. This is an editorial preference with concrete validation in the editor, not a claim that every discovered game can supply those shots.

The voice keeps expressive disbelief, fictional POV, casual trash talk and obviously fictional satire. A Diddy-style game can be considered because making that recognizable figure playable is itself an absurd premise. An existence reaction does not need a real-world accusation. Do not invent allegations or sexual/minor jokes. Nor should a critic erase a harmless gaming reaction simply because the player has no visible face. Literal hours played,14attempts, repeated losses or specific outcomes still require actual evidence.
