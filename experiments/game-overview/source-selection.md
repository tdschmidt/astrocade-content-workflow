# Game-overview source selection

Selected **40 Ways to Kill a Zombie**, an existing 175.044-second portrait recording. This format explains the game that is visible, so the selection prioritizes several demonstrated mechanics and their consequences. It does not need an uninterrupted traversal route.

| Candidate | Observed material | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 40 Ways to Kill a Zombie | A central target and visible tool bar; sword impact and dismemberment, fire scorching/stagger, bomb explosion, acid exposing a skeleton. Four chronological windows from one recording. | Select. Large readable effects, a coherent premise, and enough distinct action for a roughly 35-second explanation. |
| Ben 10 | Existing two long exploration captures show transformations and separate flight bursts, with menus, inference pauses, occasional building occlusion and featureless ground between them. | Good future movement/abilities overview, but requires more discontinuous source selection and careful claims about each form. The zombie source offers clearer consecutive demonstrations within this experiment. |
| Iron Golem | Several tumbling, impact and gem-collection episodes. | Strong for the parallel longer troll montage; narrower mechanic variety for an overview. |
| Stickman Archer | One short 8.166-second successful shot, fall and victory sequence. | Too little unique footage for the requested longer overview without artificial extension. |
| Rail Journey | New train traversal recording with throttle, horn and braking controls observed. | Usable story background. Less varied demonstrated interaction than the four zombie tools; do not confuse the two formats. |

Prior comparison evidence lives in `experiments/troll-editor/source-selection.md` and `experiments/story-background/source-selection.md`. New source sheets in `data/experiments/game-overview/evidence/` show sword, fire and acid at 2 FPS. The bomb was already inspected at 2 FPS and 8 FPS in the troll experiment. The actual overview agent additionally received timestamped 2 FPS source images across every allowed chapter; those exact JPEGs and mappings are retained with each draft run.

The [source ledger](source-ledger.json) records the raw path, SHA-256, allowed windows, visible facts and forbidden claims. Codex selected 40–49.5s (sword), 67.5–75.5s (fire), 138–145.2s (bomb), and 165–174.5s (acid). There is no repeated footage. These are four tests, not a claim of continuous play or a flawless win streak.

Fire changes the character's appearance and posture but does not visibly advance the counter. The narration describes the shown reaction without claiming an elimination. The advertised title is not evidence that all forty methods have been tested. No personal playtime, multiplayer, campaign or unseen progression is claimed.

Initial scouting suggested the empty upper-middle wall for captions, but actual-output review found a brief overlap with the bomb-launched torso. Final v2 uses three-word lower-middle captions, leaving the moving torso and standing face readable. Some particles and the final skeleton approach the text; the visible effect remains understandable. Literal center placement, although common in the user's reference, would obscure this game's target and effects. The crop removes the Astrocade page footer. The source's own in-game counter/tool labels remain; no game-name watermark or source-credit bar is added.
