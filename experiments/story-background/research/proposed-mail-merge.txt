How much paperwork would you do to save printer ink? A Reddit user described an office that filled out repair forms by hand, even though most details repeated. They suggested printing the forms from a spreadsheet. The manager rejected it because of the ink. So they kept writing until their last day. Then they spent twenty minutes building the spreadsheet. In under half an hour, the usual morning paperwork for three people was printed. They dropped the stack on the manager's desk, took a tea break that lasted through lunch, collected their things, and left. My verdict? Expensive way to save ink.
