# Velocity / aura accent

Editorial grammar: compressed approach → brief slowdown at a real move or transformation → impact on the exact visible payoff → immediate clean continuation. This is a rhythm-focused style, not evidence of player skill.

- Duration guidance: 6–10 seconds, preferably one coherent action rather than unrelated highlights.
- Use 1.3–2× speed on uneventful approach, 0.6–0.8× briefly around the real action, and return to normal to show consequence. Use 3–5 clips, preserve chronological continuity, and avoid oscillating speeds without purpose.
- Use the `velocity` demonstration bed. Align the drop and one impact or whoosh with the visible payoff. One short punch is enough.
- Keep mostly clean color. Optional black-and-white can provide a very short anticipation beat; deep fry is not this style's signature. A freeze is optional and must be shorter than 0.4 seconds.
- Usually omit the face. If the actual event is absurd rather than impressive, one small face can acknowledge that irony. At most two terse captions, and never add unsupported “clutch”, “perfect”, or “win” claims.
