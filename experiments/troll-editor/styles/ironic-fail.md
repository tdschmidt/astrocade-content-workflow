# Ironic fail / deep-fried interruption

Editorial grammar: confident setup → obvious mistake → record-stop/grayscale pause → brief ugly deep-fried reaction → actual recovery or unresolved cost. The joke is the contradiction between confidence and the observed mistake.

- Duration guidance: 15–25 seconds. Show enough lead-in to recognize the better available choice when the source provides one.
- Use one short, clearly ironic setup caption; no claims of a win or optimal decision.
- Rank all visible reactions before assigning effects. A character withstanding an early attack is a setup beat when a later attack creates the decisive reversal. That early beat can have one restrained cue or short clean hold, but save the dominant freeze/color change/punch and bass for the actual climax.
- At the decisive reversal, retain visible contact before an optional short grayscale hold. Deep fry at most roughly 0.3–0.6 seconds; attach the reaction face directly to the actual frozen head as one distinct interruption. Omit the face if the head is already destroyed or off-screen; do not select a weaker event merely to accommodate a sticker.
- Resume the real gameplay; accelerate uneventful travel up to 2× but preserve the approach/contact and result of any recovery. Do not turn recovery into an invented full success.
- Use real meme sound files and a reviewed phonk excerpt from the supplied catalog; no procedural demonstration audio. Align its main drop with the decisive reversal and strongest visual treatment. The resumption after a minor early hold must not accidentally become the main drop. Two or three cues suffice.
- Place the climax late enough that the build has somewhere to go, then let the drop play over the readable consequence for roughly 4–6 seconds and finish on a resolved musical beat/phrase. Retain moving aftermath and the real counter or final state that proves the result. Do not stretch an empty aftermath or a freeze; select a source with enough interesting consequence.
- Keep arithmetic/score text readable in clean footage before and after the gag. Avoid zoom when it would crop that evidence. A short final caption may acknowledge the actual limited recovery.
